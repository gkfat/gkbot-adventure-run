PIXEL ART STYLE

Camera:
- 3/4 view
- slightly elevated camera
- consistent orientation

Geometry:
- visible top and side planes
- exaggerated thickness
- readable silhouettes

Lighting:
- single global light source
- upper-left
- top surfaces brighter
- side surfaces darker
- undersides darkest

Shading:
- 3–5 discrete value steps
- hard pixel clusters
- no smooth gradients

Depth:
- contact shadows
- ambient occlusion
- cast shadows
- overlapping elements
- foreground/background separation

Materials:
- different materials use different pixel clusters
- metal = sharp highlights
- wood = irregular clusters
- stone = broad clusters
- cloth = soft irregular clusters

Pixel construction:
- intentional pixel clusters
- no anti-aliasing
- no subpixel detail
- no vector-like edges
- no excessive outlines

Composition:
- strong silhouette
- readable at native resolution
- avoid UI-icon appearance
- avoid flat sticker appearance
